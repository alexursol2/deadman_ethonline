# ETHGlobal Project Check-in #1

**Project:** Deadman — an x402 escrow whose refund is executed by the network, not by a keeper.
**Submitted:** 2026-09-08. **Track:** Hedera, AI & Agentic Payments. Second partner: Privy.

---

## Text to paste

> **Progress.** Hedera testnet is doing what the design needs. We verified on-chain that a HIP-1215
> scheduled call fires unattended (schedule `0.0.10395748`, executed 4 ms into its target second,
> `signatures: []`), that a contract can cancel its own pending schedule, and that no third party —
> including the account that deployed the contract — can cancel it. Six spikes, all evidence
> committed with mirror-node JSON and HashScan links. The contract design plan is written and under
> review today; `HoldEscrow.sol` starts after that.
>
> **Blocker 1 — unanswered Hedera questions.** We have four findings from testnet that need a Hedera
> engineer's answer, and we have not had one yet. (a) A rejected `deleteSchedule` returns transaction
> status SUCCESS with the refusal only in the return code — a contract that discards it can pay out
> twice, and Hedera's own `payments-scheduler` scaffold template ships a helper that discards it.
> (b) `scheduleCall` reverts with empty returndata when starved of gas, which contradicts how
> HIP-1215's "never reverts" reads, and the gas floor is ~1.45M. (c) The delete authorisation model
> is undocumented; we established it empirically. (d) `hasScheduleCapacity(now + 1)` returns false
> despite the HIP stating a one-second minimum. We would like these in front of Hedera before we
> finalise the contract, since two of them affect its structure.
>
> **Blocker 2 — Blocky402 is a single-vendor hard gate.** Settling through the Blocky402 facilitator
> is a stated track requirement. It is one hosted service, `api.testnet.blocky402.com`, with no
> public source repository and no published status page or SLA that we can find. It is up and
> responding correctly today. If it goes down or changes during the build week we cannot self-host a
> substitute and still satisfy the requirement, and we would fail qualification for reasons entirely
> outside our control. We are not asking for the requirement to change — we are flagging that our
> track qualification has a single point of failure we cannot mitigate, and asking whether there is a
> contact or a fallback position if it becomes unavailable.
>
> **Related, and worth Hedera knowing.** The official `scaffold-hbar` "x402 pay per use" template
> defaults its facilitator to a self-hosted service on `localhost:4020`. Blocky402 appears once in
> the whole template, in `RUNBOOK.md`, as an optional fallback. A team following the template's happy
> path settles through their own facilitator and **fails the track requirement with every test
> passing and nothing erroring**. One environment variable fixes it. We caught it on day one; other
> teams may not, and will not find out until judging.
>
> **Not blocked on:** anything of our own. Repo is public with real commit history, deployment is on
> testnet, and the team is on schedule for a Saturday submission.
